RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

RAG systems retrieve relevant information from a knowledge base and provide
that information to a language model as context.

The main stages of a RAG system are document loading, chunking, embedding,
retrieval, and generation.

Embeddings are numerical representations of text. Similar pieces of text
have similar embeddings.

A vector database can efficiently search for embeddings that are similar
to a user's question.

Chunking is the process of splitting large documents into smaller pieces.
Good chunk sizes help retrieval find relevant information without adding
too much unnecessary context.
